AutoCare – Car Maintenance Management System

AutoCare is a web-based platform designed to help car owners manage and track their vehicle maintenance efficiently. Users can register their cars, monitor maintenance history, schedule regular services, and receive reminders for important tasks such as oil changes and periodic inspections. The platform aims to simplify vehicle management, prevent unexpected breakdowns, and help users keep their cars in optimal condition.
